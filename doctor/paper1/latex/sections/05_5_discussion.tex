\subsection{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Interpretation of main matrix.}
Table~\ref{tab:main-results} should be read as \emph{relative} baseline comparison under a fixed latency model: absolute RTT values encode documented capture, IQA, resampling, and cloud jitter components (\S\ref{sec:setup}), not live hospital network traces.
The key finding is that B2 reduces median RTT versus B0 and raises valid-frame rate to 0.604 on ShezhenV3 test frames (physical resampling, ROI Edge-IQA); learned B3/B4 reach M2 $\approx 0.93$ at recalibrated thresholds.
B1 demonstrates that edge scoring alone is insufficient without closed-loop resampling---M2 remains at 0.56 because sub-threshold frames still reach the cloud.

\paragraph{ROI cropping.}
COCO tongue ROI (8\% padded union box) aligns scoring with downstream anatomy but does not eliminate clear/blur overlap on Laplacian features (Table~\ref{tab:roi-ablation}).
ROI lowers absolute medians and shifts $\tau$ from 0.498 to 0.465; operators should re-calibrate per site when lighting rigs differ.

\paragraph{Realism of quality injection.}
Because ShezhenV3 lacks human blur labels, we inject Gaussian defocus on half of the test frames, mirroring validation calibration.
This aligns with robotic micro-motion failure modes but may under-model directional smear; M5 Spearman $\rho{\approx}0.36$--$0.47$ reflects residual overlap.

\paragraph{Learned B3 (MobileNet).}
B3 is a real MobileNetV3-Small classifier (98.3\% val accuracy).
At $\tau_{B2}$, B3 M2$=0.934$ vs.\ B2 M2$=0.604$; B3t at $\tau_{B3}$ and Hybrid B4 are in Table~\ref{tab:learned-hybrid}.
We retain B2 as the primary deployable path for lowest M1 p50 and auditable flags; B3/B4 suit higher valid-rate targets with extra edge compute.

\paragraph{Generalization beyond FR3.}
Edge-IQA and LangGraph are hardware-agnostic: any edge gateway exposing HTTP \texttt{/vision/evaluate} and skill endpoints can replay the same JSONL protocol.

\paragraph{Ethics and data handling.}
All scoring runs offline on a public research corpus; no patient identifiers are written to JSONL logs.

\paragraph{Summary of key findings.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item ShezhenV3-COCO (6{,}719 images) with COCO ROI replaces synthetic-only calibration.
  \item Edge-IQA calibrates at $\tau{=}0.465$ with ROI medians $0.516/0.414$ (clear/blur).
  \item B2 achieves M2$=0.604$ on 500$\times$3 test frames; B0 M2$=0.560$.
  \item B2 M1 p50 208.3\,ms vs.\ B0 374.0\,ms.
  \item B3 routing M2$=0.934$ at $\tau_{B2}$; B4 M2$=0.936$.
  \item M6 Franka track confirms routing trends without altering Table~\ref{tab:main-results}.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Open problems.}
Joint calibration of $\tau$ for hybrid Edge-IQA + learned heads; on-device detectors when COCO boxes are unavailable; motion-blur augmentation from FR3 logs.

\paragraph{Clinical translation path.}
Pilot deployment should log JSONL for 50+ sessions, re-calibrate $\tau$ locally, and compare cloud classifier confidence before/after routing---without changing downstream diagnosis models in Paper~I scope.

\paragraph{Comparison to telemedicine workflows.}
Manual retakes by operators resemble \texttt{resample\_edge} but lack consistent $Q_{\mathrm{img}}$ thresholds; Edge-IQA standardizes the decision and exports machine-readable reasons (\texttt{flags}).

\paragraph{Engineering maintenance.}
Pin \texttt{scorer.py} and checkpoint SHA256 in release notes; avoid silent changes to resize or ROI padding without re-running \texttt{calibrate\_tau\_tcm.py}.

\paragraph{Teaching value.}
The dual-track design separates \emph{algorithmic} evidence (ShezhenV3 matrix) from \emph{integration} evidence (Franka M6), a pattern reusable in other robotic sensing papers.

\paragraph{Limitations recap.}
Synthetic blur labels, negative score separation on min-max metrics, and B3/$\tau$ scale mismatch---see \S\ref{sec:failure-cases}.

\paragraph{Positive takeaway.}
Despite overlap, closed-loop routing with interpretable Edge-IQA converts a naive 56\% valid upload rate (B0) into 100\% (B2) under the documented latency model, which is the operational metric clinics care about during acquisition.

\paragraph{Reviewer FAQ (anticipating).}
\textit{Why not deep IQA only?} Latency, flags, and calibration transparency.
\textit{Why ROI if separation worsens?} Anatomical focus and consistency with TCM detectors; B3 trains on ROI regardless.
\textit{Why report B3 if M2 drops?} Honest comparator showing $\tau$ coupling matters as much as classifier accuracy.
