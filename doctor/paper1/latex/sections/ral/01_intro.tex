\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Tele-diagnosis for traditional Chinese medicine (TCM) tongue inspection needs repeatable, high-quality images before cloud analytics and electronic medical records integration~\cite{zhang2020tongue,qi2016tongue}.
Clinical diagnosis relies on color, texture, coating, and shape cues that degrade under motion blur, uneven lighting, and incorrect working distance.
Naive pipelines upload every frame, wasting bandwidth on blurred or poorly exposed captures and inflating round-trip time (RTT) on slow links.
\emph{Garbage-in garbage-out} effects are acute: a single blurred upload triggers wasted RTT and may propagate low-confidence features into downstream classifiers.
Edge-side gating must be (i)~fast for closed-loop capture, (ii)~interpretable for audit, and (iii)~calibratable on real tongue corpora rather than synthetic placeholders alone.

We study a \textbf{cloud--edge--device} stack for a Franka Research~3 (FR3) platform with ROS~2 and MoveIt~\cite{moore2021ros2,chitta2009moveit}.
Perception and routing live in a Python edge gateway (\texttt{franka\_api\_server}) and companion edge modules (\texttt{edge\_iqa}, \texttt{langgraph\_router})---not inside hardware plugins---so algorithms remain testable without a robot.
A lightweight \emph{Edge-IQA} fuses Laplacian sharpness and exposure into $Q_{\mathrm{img}}\in[0,1]$ on COCO tongue ROI crops; a LangGraph router decides \texttt{upload\_cloud}, \texttt{resample\_edge}, or \texttt{fail\_safe} under retry budget $K{=}2$.
An optional learned baseline B3 fine-tunes MobileNetV3-Small on the training split for comparison.

We validate on public \textbf{ShezhenV3-COCO} (6{,}719 images with bounding boxes): $\tau$ is calibrated on 572 validation pairs (clear + synthetic blur) with ROI enabled, and the main matrix replays 500 test frames per seed with real Edge-IQA and B3 scores from 553 held-out images.

\textbf{Contributions:} (1)~Edge-IQA with tongue ROI---$O(HW)$ CPU scoring, interpretable blur/exposure flags, and p95 below 10\,ms;
(2)~auditable LangGraph three-branch routing logged as JSONL for clinical audit trails;
(3)~dual-track evaluation---offline baselines B0--B4 on ShezhenV3 (3 seeds) plus an auxiliary Franka closed-loop track (M6) confirming execution-stack feasibility without altering Table~\ref{tab:main-results}.

Section~\ref{sec:related} reviews related work; \S\ref{sec:method}--\ref{sec:experiments} present the method and ShezhenV3 evaluation; \S\ref{sec:conclusion} concludes.
