\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Robotic medical imaging.}
ROS~2 and MoveIt enable repeatable manipulator-mounted cameras~\cite{moore2021ros2,chitta2009moveit}.
We adopt named \emph{skills} (\texttt{go\_to\_tongue\_pose}, \texttt{go\_to\_face\_pose}) instead of online impedance tuning, matching clinical repeatability while keeping Paper~I independent of force-control threads.

\paragraph{Image quality assessment.}
\textbf{Full-reference} metrics (PSNR, SSIM) require pristine references unavailable per patient.
\textbf{Hand-crafted NR-IQA} (Laplacian, BRISQUE, NIQE~\cite{mittal2012niqe}) runs on CPU but seldom exposes blur/exposure semantics for audit logs.
\textbf{Deep NR-IQA} (HyperIQA~\cite{su2020hyperiqa}, CLIP-IQA~\cite{wang2023clipiqa}) improves MOS correlation at GPU cost and limited interpretability.
Edge closed-loop capture favors linear-time operators with auditable failure modes; Edge-IQA follows this line with explicit flags and calibrated $\tau$ (Table~\ref{tab:iqa-compare}).

\paragraph{Edge--cloud orchestration.}
Split inference reduces bandwidth in mobile vision~\cite{kang2017neurosurgeon,zhou2019edge}; Zhou et al.~\cite{zhou2019edge} taxonomize edge intelligence tiers.
We gate \emph{upload decisions} before heavy cloud models execute, using a lightweight scorer (p95 $\approx$9.6\,ms CPU) rather than partial DNN migration.
LangGraph-style routers express branching policies with logs; ReAct~\cite{yao2023react} motivates interleaving tool actions with routing.

\paragraph{TCM tongue analysis and positioning.}
Cloud TCM pipelines~\cite{zhang2020tongue,qi2016tongue} assume usable inputs; ShezhenV3-COCO supplies boxes for \emph{downstream} classifiers.
Paper~I addresses \emph{acquisition quality and routing} upstream on the full public corpus.
Compared with generic edge offloading, we target robotic \emph{capture} loops where quality varies shot-to-shot; compared with NR-IQA literature, we prioritize thresholdable $Q_{\mathrm{img}}$ and JSONL auditability aligned with medical device software trends.
